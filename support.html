Client Contact Log — Help & Support

A record of every contact with the client, on the Trello card.

Getting started

  1. Open a card and scroll to the Client contact log section.
  2. Click + Log a client contact. Set the time, the channel and who initiated the contact, and write a short summary.
  3. Click Save record (or press Ctrl+Enter). The newest record is shown first.

Use ✎ to change a record and ✕ (click twice) to delete it.

Seeing who needs a call

  • The front of each card shows how long ago the last contact was, for example "Contact 12d ago". It turns red once it passes the number of days set in Settings.
  • The Client contact button at the top of the board (it may be inside the board's Power-Ups menu) lists every card with a log, longest silence first, and counts the contacts made in the last 7 days. Export CSV gives you every record on the board for a spreadsheet. On a board with hundreds of cards the list takes a few seconds to load.

Settings

Board menu → Power-Ups → Client Contact Log → Settings: language (English or 简体中文), the number of days before a card is flagged (0 turns flagging off), which channels to offer and your own extra channels, and lists that are never flagged, such as finished work.

Frequently asked questions

How many records fit on a card? Trello allows about 4,000 characters of Power-Up data per card. That is roughly 50 records with short summaries, about 30 with summaries of 100 characters, and about 24 with summaries of 130 English characters. The section shows the space used and warns from 85%.

The card is full. What now? Click Archive old records. The oldest records are shown as text: copy them into your own client file, tick the box to confirm, then remove them from the card. The card keeps a count of archived records. Removal cannot be undone.

Who can see the records? Board members with edit access. Other viewers see no badge and no log. Note that Trello stores Power-Up data so that anyone able to view the board can technically read it, so do not use this on a public board.

Does it work in the Trello mobile app? No. Trello's mobile apps do not show sections added by Power-Ups. Use Trello in a web browser.

Does it send reminders? No. A Power-Up only runs while a board is open in a browser. Use the badges and the overview.

Does it read my email or phone? No. Records are typed in by hand. Nothing is connected to any other service.

I was typing and the section reloaded. Trello reloads Power-Up sections when a card's data changes. Your unsaved text is restored automatically.

Is this my official client file? No. It is a working log with limited space. Keep whatever records your profession requires in your own system.
