Client Contact Log — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Client Contact Log is a Power-Up for Trello published by ZHIJIEVISA PTY LTD (ABN 89 167 335 257), Suite 3, Level 6, 68 Grenfell St, Adelaide ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to information when you use it.

Summary

  • Everything you enter is stored in your own Trello board, using Trello's Power-Up data storage.
  • We do not run a server for this Power-Up. We do not receive, see, copy or store your board data.
  • No cookies, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking are used by the Power-Up.

What the Power-Up stores, and where

When you log a contact, the Power-Up saves the following on the Trello card it belongs to: the date and time you entered, the channel (for example phone or email), who initiated the contact, the summary you typed, and the Trello full name of the board member who logged it. When records are archived, the card also keeps a count of archived records and the date of the newest one. Board-level settings (language, the number of days before a card is flagged, channels and lists that are never flagged) are saved on the Trello board.

This information is held by Trello (Atlassian) as part of your board, under your agreement with Atlassian and the Atlassian Privacy Policy. It is stored with Trello's "shared" visibility, which means anyone who can view the board in Trello can read it, including through Trello's API. Do not use the Power-Up on a public board.

Summaries are free text. What you write about your own clients or contacts is your responsibility, including any privacy or confidentiality obligations you owe them.

What is kept in your browser

While you are typing a record, the unsaved draft is kept in your browser's session storage so that it is not lost if Trello reloads the card. It never leaves your browser, it is removed when the record is saved or cancelled, and the browser discards it when the tab is closed.

What we receive

Nothing from your boards. The Power-Up runs entirely in your browser and talks only to Trello.

If you contact us for support, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to send. We use it only to reply to you and to fix problems. We keep support correspondence only for as long as we need it to handle your request and for our ordinary business records, and we will delete it earlier if you ask us to, unless the law requires us to keep it.

Third parties involved in delivering the Power-Up

  • Trello (Atlassian) stores your data and loads the Power-Up inside Trello. The Power-Up also loads Trello's Power-Up client library from Trello's servers.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the Power-Up's program files (HTML, JavaScript, CSS). When your browser downloads these files, GitHub may log technical information such as your IP address, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. No board data is sent to GitHub.

Deleting your data

You can delete any record from the card at any time (the ✕ button on the record), and "Archive old records" removes the oldest records after you have copied them. Deleting or archiving a card, or removing the Power-Up's data when you disable it, is handled by Trello. Because we hold none of your board data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Not a complete file record

Trello limits how much a Power-Up can store on a card, and records are entered by hand. The log is a working aid. It is not a substitute for any client file or record you are required to keep.
